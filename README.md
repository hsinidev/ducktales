### Game Details & Guide
Getting Started with DuckTales


DuckTales is a browser-based game that allows players to explore, collect items, and engage in various activities with Scrooge McDuck, Huey, Dewey, and Louie, his great-nephews. The objective of the game is to help the Duck family accumulate wealth by collecting rare coins and gems while navigating through levels.


How to Play


To play DuckTales, follow these steps:

1. Open a web browser and navigate to the game's main page.
2. Click on the "Start" button to begin the adventure.
3. Use the arrow keys or mouse to control Scrooge McDuck as he navigates through levels.
4. Collect rare coins and gems by clicking on them to pick them up.
5. Avoid obstacles such as wild ducks, enemies, and traps.
6. Upgrade your weapons and abilities as you progress through levels.


Tips and Tricks for Success


To increase your chances of success in DuckTales, try these tips:
• Master the controls: Practice navigating Scrooge McDuck's movements to avoid obstacles and collect items efficiently.
• Upgrade wisely: Choose upgrades that suit the current level and your progress so far.
• Explore thoroughly: Don't be afraid to explore each level to find hidden treasures and secret areas.
• Use your environment: Utilize the environment to your advantage, such as ladders, stairs, and obstacles to defeat enemies or avoid danger.
• Keep an eye on your health: Manage Scrooge McDuck's health to avoid losing it when facing tough enemies or traps.
